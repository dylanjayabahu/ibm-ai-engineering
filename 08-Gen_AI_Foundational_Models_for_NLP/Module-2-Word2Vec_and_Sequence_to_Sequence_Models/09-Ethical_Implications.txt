Ethical Implications of Word Embeddings & LMs

- Bias
  - W2V/embeddings capture stereotypes (e.g., gender + jobs).
  - Risks: biased outputs, unfair automation.
  - Fixes: debias algos, fairness eval, ongoing checks.

- Privacy
  - Train data may leak sensitive info.
  - Fixes: data anon, differential privacy (DP), informed consent, transparency.

- Fair Representation
  - Risk: underperformance for under-rep groups.
  - Fixes: diverse data, inclusive metrics, continuous monitoring/updates.

- Conclusion
  - Key: reduce bias, protect privacy, ensure inclusivity.
  - Need: transparency + accountability → trustworthy AI.
