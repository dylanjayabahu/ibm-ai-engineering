PEFT = Parameter Efficient Fine Tuning 

SFT = supervised fine tuning 
    adapting previous knowledge from training to a new task 

Full fine tuning (FFT)
    supdate learning parameters,layers, neurons of LLM
    Requires lots of comp resources and memory
    Needs lots of task-specific data
    RIsk of overfitting
    Takes long time
    Complex to implemenent 
    Prone to catastrophig forgetting; forgetting prelearned model 



PEFT overcomes the issues with FFT
    reduces total number of trainable params to be updated 
    decrease total comp resources and memory 


PEFT Methods:
    selective fine tuning 
    additive fine tuning 
    reparameterization fine tuning 

    ^ all more stable than full fine tuning 


Selective fine tuning 
    select on a susbet of all params to update; e.g. just the last few layers or last layer 

Additive fine tuning 
    we dont modify the existing arch but add an extra layer or two onto the end for our task 
    additional layers are trained and pretrained portion is fixed 

    u can inject extra layers anywhere (doesnt have to be just at the end)
        ^ e.g. adapters add an extra new layer b/w the feed-forward and add-norm skip connections of a standard transformer block
        with adapters u can use a transformer and just insert adapters in for ur specific task, updating only the adapter neurons 

    Soft prompts = type of additive fine tuning 
        they are learnable tensors concatenated with input embeddings; instead of fine tuning the arch we fine tune the embeddings
        
        Methods
            prompt tuning
            prefix tuning 
            P-tuning
            multitask prompt tuning


        Prefix Tuning 
            e.g. u want to train from general chatbot to medical chatbot
            simply append parameter embeddings to the usual embeddings
            train model by freezing all params except embeddings

Repaarameterization fine tuning 
    Recall rank from lin alg (the minimum num vectors needed to span a space); aka the dimension 

    The input/output dims of a neural net have fixed dimensions

    BUT we can use lower rank operationsto reduce the num parameters
        essentially a temporary dimension reduction 
    
    makes model more efficient 

    LoRA = Low Rank Adapatation (uses reparameterixing network weights; low rank transformations)
        Instead of going from a layer with 10 neurons to a layer with 8 neurons (requiring 80 parameters)
        we can go from 10-3-8, which requires (10x3+3x8=54 parameters)

    Other methods
        LoRA (see above)
        Quantied Low Rank Adapatation (QLoRA)
            combines Lora with quantization; lowerm emory footprint and computationr equirements
        Weight decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation (DoRA)
            adjust rank of the low rank spaces based on component magnitude
            optimizes performance and efficiency
