### Ethical Considerations in Fine-Tuning Large Language Models (LLMs)

#### 1. Bias Amplification
- **Issue:** Training data may contain societal biases that models can amplify.
- **Mitigation:** Use debiasing techniques (e.g. embedding adjustments, data filtering) and regularly monitor outputs.

#### 2. Data Privacy
- **Issue:** Models may memorize and leak sensitive or proprietary information.
- **Mitigation:** Apply **differential privacy** and **data anonymization** during fine-tuning.

#### 3. Environmental Impact
- **Issue:** Training and fine-tuning LLMs consume significant compute and energy.
- **Mitigation:** Use **energy-efficient methods** (e.g. PEFT, model distillation) and **carbon offset initiatives**.

#### 4. Transparency & Accountability
- **Issue:** Lack of clarity in model sources and limitations can lead to misuse.
- **Mitigation:** Maintain **clear documentation** and **usage guidelines**.

#### 5. Fair Representation
- **Issue:** Underrepresented groups may be excluded or misrepresented.
- **Mitigation:** Ensure **dataset diversity** and **regular fairness evaluations**.
