Data Quality = accuracy, consistency, and completeness of ds used for training 
    poor quality data adds noise; lowers model accuracy and reliability 

    improving data quality 
        noise reduction = remove irrelvant noise or repetitive data; e.g. remove types, forum tags, etc. from a ds
        consistency checks = very consistency to prevent conflicting labels or outdaded info confusing model; e.g. technical terms, public figure names must be consistently used in ds 
        labeling quality = make sure labels are accurate; clear guidelines for human annotators
