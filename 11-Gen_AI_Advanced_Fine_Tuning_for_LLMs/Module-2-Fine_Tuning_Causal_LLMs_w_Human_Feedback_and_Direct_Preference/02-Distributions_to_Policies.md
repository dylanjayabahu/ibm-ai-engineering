Policies in RL
- 
- Def: a strategy or mapping that an agent uses to decide its actions based on the current state of the environment 
- They define distributions over possible actions; dictates how sequences of actions are generated
- Use in RL: Helps agnets perform tasks effectively e.g. playing game by choosing actions that get u to a goal 

Policies in LLMS
- 
- LLM can also be seen as having policies
    - Model generates text based on policy distribution rather than deterministically choosing the most likely next token 
    - Lets u explore (recall exploration vs exploitation from RL) of multiple text paths leading to diverse/contextually good outputs
- Makes creative, diverse responses
- Adapts to new contexts
- Improves decision making in text tasks


Relationship between Policies and LMs
- 
- Formally, $y \sim \pi(y|x)$, where $x$ is input sequence, $y$ is output sequence, and $\pi$ is policy (probability distribution over outputs)
- Probability of each token $y$ depends on previous tokens (just like distribution based approach)

Rollouts
- 
- Rollout = single sampled response generated by the model for a given query 
- E.g. For the query: "What is the largest ocean?", a possible rollout could be "Pacific Ocean" or "The Pacific Ocean is the largest.", etc
- Each rollout is one sequnce sampled based on the policy; one path
- In RL, rollouts involve rewards to evaluate the actions
- But in LLM libraries, rollouts are just diff sample response w/o rewards

Basically we are connecting the distribution from prev lesson to the Policies that agents use in RL
The LLM is an agent exploring diff text sequences.
