Reward Modeling
    technique used to help llms produce responses that better match human preferneces
    instead of just predicting next word, we teach model what humans prefer as good responses

    Accomplished by:
        quantifying quality (assign numerical score)
        aligning with humans (reward score reflects human feedback)
        model is fined tuned to maximize reward scores
        ensures consistent; model becomes more reliable and consistent over time 
    
Example of Reward Modelling
    If reward model is trained to prefer cats;
    then "Cats are amazing" results in a high score while "Cats are ok" results in a low score
    The model learns to favor cat-loving responses 



Response Evaluation = how we measure which response is better on some criteria
    E.g. IF the question is "which country owns antarctica", then we have two responses:
        "antarctica is governed by the antarctic treaty system" = high score e.g. 0.89
         and 
         "Penguin overlords rule antarctica" = low score, e.g. 0.03

    The way we do that is we read the query and response together; if they fit its a good response if they dont fit its a bad response
        We tokenize both the query and response tokens, whch get fed into the reward model to compute a score
